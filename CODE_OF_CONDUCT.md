# Code of Conduct

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1, which is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind and patient with other people.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assuming good intent and asking questions before drawing conclusions.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments, in public or in private.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not follow this code of conduct, and will explain the reason when appropriate.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces, and when an individual is officially representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers by using GitHub's private reporting features on this repository, or by contacting the maintainer listed on their GitHub profile. All reports are reviewed promptly and handled with discretion. Maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of the person who reports an incident.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will follow these steps, in proportion to the behavior:

1. **Correction.** A private, written warning with an explanation of the violation.
2. **Warning.** A warning with consequences for continued behavior, including a period without interaction with the people involved.
3. **Temporary ban.** A temporary ban from the project's spaces for a defined period.
4. **Permanent ban.** A permanent ban for a pattern of violations or serious harassment.
